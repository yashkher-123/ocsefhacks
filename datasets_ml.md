# sample datasets for your project

pick a dataset that fits your skill level. HARDER DATASET != BETTER PROJECT

---

## beginner

clean, all numeric, easy to handle. good for first model.

https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/294/combined+cycle+power+plant -- power plant output

https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/242/energy+efficiency -- energy efficiency of buildings

https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/551/gas+turbine+co+and+nox+emission+data+set -- gas turbine emmisions

https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/374/appliances+energy+prediction -- home appliances energy use

https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/162/forest+fires -- fire prediction

https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/17/breast+cancer+wisconsin+diagnostic -- breast cancer prediction

https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/267/banknote+authentication -- banknote authentication

https://scikit-learn.org/stable/datasets/toy_dataset.html -- built-in scikitlearn simple datasets



---

## intermediate

missing data, categorical vars, bigger size, etc most might need cleaning/data engineering first

https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/381/beijing+pm2+5+data -- pollution prediction

https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/360/air+quality -- air quality

https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/berkeleyearth/climate-change-earth-surface-temperature-data -- climate change

https://scikit-learn.org/stable/datasets/real_world.html -- built-in skl medium datasets

https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/kumargh/pimaindiansdiabetescsv -- diabetes prediction

https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mlg-ulb/creditcardfraud/data -- credit card fraud (WATCH FOR IMBALANCE)

---

## advanced

not always tabular. time-series, images, etc.

https://github.com/phelber/EuroSAT -- land class classification

https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/fantineh/next-day-wildfire-spread - wildfire prediction (images)

https://bigearth.net/ -- big datasets on earth data

https://github.com/ultralytics/ultralytics -- fine tune a yolo model

https://ollama.com/library/phi3 -- fine tune an llm (use unsloth and LoRA in colab for best efficiency)

https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/land-based-station/global-historical-climatology-network-daily -- time-series climate data

https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/paultimothymooney/chest-xray-pneumonia -- x-ray classifications
